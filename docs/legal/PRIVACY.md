# Dewy — Privacy Policy (draft — have counsel review before publish)

**Last updated:** 2026-09-21

Dewy helps you organize beauty and skincare products you already own.

## Data we store on your device
Routine preferences, cabinet products you add, and journal notes may be stored locally on your device.

## Data we do not sell
We do not sell your personal information.

## Search
Product search in early builds may run entirely on your device against a built-in list. Nothing you type in search is sent to Phil or Morgan’s servers unless a future approved cloud feature says so in an updated policy.
