# Dewy Founder Decisions — Locked

Recorded 2026-09-21. These decisions are locked and govern all Dewy planning and implementation phases. Changes require an explicit founder instruction.

## Platform And Market

- **Launch Platform:** iOS first, with platform-independent backend services.
- **Existing Code:** Audit before changing frameworks or architecture.
- **Launch Market:** United States only.

## Product Classification And Safety

- **Product Classification:** Cosmetic routine and self-care guidance; no diagnosis or treatment.
- **Expert Governance:** Board-certified dermatologist and cosmetic chemist review the knowledge and safety systems; no individual clinical review at launch.
- **Minimum User Age:** 18.
- **Excluded At Launch:** Diagnoses, prescriptions, cycle data, pregnancy data, exact location, health-platform integrations, biometric identification, and beauty scoring.

## Business Model

- **Business Model:** Freemium subscription. No commerce at launch.
- **Paid Plan:** Dewy Pro at $12.99/month or $99/year, subject to pricing validation.

## Data And Trust

- **Ingredient Sources:** Product label first, manufacturer verification second, canonical INCI normalization, approved authoritative sources, and expert-reviewed explanations.
- **Product Images:** Temporary processing by default; maximum 30-day retention after verification unless the user elects to save them.
- **Skin Images:** Optional, encrypted, user-controlled, excluded from advertising, identity recognition, and model training.
- **Sensitive Data:** Collect only information necessary for cosmetic routine personalization.

## Scope And Schedule

- **Launch Scope:** Product Capture, Your Cabinet, AM/PM Routine Generation, Completion, Modification, Skin-Comfort Check-In, and Factual Timeline.
- **Launch Schedule:** Private alpha target 2026-12-13 (12 weeks from 2026-09-21); public U.S. beta target 2027-02-07 (20 weeks), pending repository audit.

## Brand And Product Principles

- **Brand Direction:** Feminine, editorial, polished, intelligent, warm, and non-clinical.
- **Interface Principle:** One useful recommendation at a time.
- **Founder Rule:** Do not add features until the core scan-to-routine loop is accurate, understandable, trusted, and repeatedly used.
