# Dewy Design Craft Blueprint

## Identity (ours)
Dewy is soft, feminine, elevated, editorial, warm, and non-clinical.
Use the full DUEY Color System (all nine colors with their locked roles).
Always Title Case labels. Always capitalize Cabinet.

## Craft bar (Oura-level — not Oura cosplay)
We borrow **quality standards** from apps like Oura Ring, not their palette or branding.

1. **One useful thing at a time** — no dense dashboards.
2. **Breathing room** — generous spacing; silence is a feature.
3. **Quiet chrome** — few borders, soft hierarchy, no SaaS clutter.
4. **Clarity over cleverness** — every tap has an obvious next step.
5. **Premium restraint** — rare accent color (Cherry Licorice) for real actions only.
6. **Calm motion** — soft transitions; nothing flashy or gimmicky.
7. **Trust by honesty** — never fake data, never medical theater.
8. **Mobile-first polish** — large touch targets, readable type, beautiful empty states.

## Do not
- Copy Oura’s dark/black aesthetic.
- Turn Dewy into a fitness tracker UI.
- Add charts, badges, or gamification that break the vanity/editorial feel.
- Crowding the Add Product to Cabinet flow with advanced search chrome.

## Screen test
If it still looks expensive and correct in ten years with the logo off, it passes.
